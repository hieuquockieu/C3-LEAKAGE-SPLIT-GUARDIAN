The Silent Production Killer: Cross-Split Data Leakage

01 / 05

🚨 Core Pain Point

In real-world computer vision (autonomous driving, traffic analytics, security multi-camera systems), datasets originate from continuous video bursts, routes, and multi-angle camera rigs.


Traditional random splitting assumes samples are independent and identically distributed (i.i.d.). In reality, adjacent frames ($t \pm 0.1s$) or multi-camera views of the exact same vehicle are scattered into both Train and Validation sets.

💸 Who Bears the Consequences?

  • ML Engineers: Suffer from "False Confidence" (Validation score is "đẹp giả" at 96-98%), believing the model generalizes.
  • Product & Operations: Deploy model to production and suffer severe 20-40% performance degradation on unseen routes/cameras.
  • Business & Safety: Massive GPU retraining re-runs, delayed vehicle releases, and catastrophic false-negative safety risks in real-world driving.

Question 2 • Failure Reproduction & Benchmark Design

Dual-Leakage Benchmark with Ground Truth Manifest

02 / 05

🎬 Leakage Type 1: Temporal Burst Overlap

High-frequency sequential video frames ($150\text{ ms}$ delta) captured by the same camera lens.


Failure Mechanism: Differing only by minor pixel jitter or slight translation. A naive random split places frame $f_0$ in Train and frame $f_1$ in Val, letting the model memorize the vehicle rather than learning general features.

🎥 Leakage Type 2: Multi-Camera Viewpoint Overlap

Synchronous capture of the identical traffic event from multiple vantage points (e.g. cam_front_left vs cam_front_right).


Failure Mechanism: Differing by perspective warp and lighting tints. Baseline pixel/pHash methods completely miss these leaks due to high bit Hamming distance!

📐 Independent Holdout Protocol

We reserve Scenes 14 through 17 as a strict Unseen Holdout Test Set with 96 captures. This provides an indisputable ground truth benchmark to test whether validation metrics are genuine or "đẹp giả".

✨ SplitGuardian Solution Architecture
